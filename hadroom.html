    Printable answer cards

    Print one set, laminate them, hand them out. Each boy gets four cards (A, B, C, D) — or share a class set. He holds up his choice; the camera reads the whole room.

    Field-tested sizing: 5" cards read to about 22 feet with a fixed camera. Bigger cards reach further (~1" of card per ~4½ ft). Print at 100% / actual size, not "fit to page".
